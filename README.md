# Landa

A student mobility intelligence tool that helps international students assess their readiness for studying and settling in Sweden.

**Live product:** [getlanda.se](https://getlanda.se)  
**Case study:** [dareabinde.com/case/landa](https://dareabinde.com/case/landa)

## Overview

Landa is a decision-support product for prospective international students who are considering, preparing for, or settling into life in Sweden. The product helps users reflect on financial readiness, location, employment, language, support systems, expectations, and personal confidence before making a consequential move.

The goal is not to predict success or failure. It is to give students a clearer, more honest view of what they may need to prepare for.

## My Role

I led the product direction, UX research, model building, service design, interface design, and implementation of the current product experience. I also used AI-assisted development to move from concept to a functional shipped product.

This project represents my interest in designing useful digital services, not just static interfaces: products that combine research, decision support, interaction design, and real-world user context.

## What It Does

- Guides users through a readiness assessment
- Adapts questions based on programme length
- Scores readiness across seven mobility dimensions
- Generates a personalised on-page report
- Lets users email themselves a fuller branded report
- Provides official resources for immigration, study, work, wellbeing, housing, and practical settlement

## Product Thinking

Landa is built around a simple question: what does a student need to understand before committing to an international study move?

The product focuses on moments where uncertainty is high and information is scattered. Rather than only presenting resources, it helps users make sense of their own situation first, then points them toward practical next steps.

## Design Notes

The current brand direction uses a restrained mobility-service language: deep navy, coral, cool neutrals, editorial cards, and a clearer hierarchy for long-form guidance. The experience is intentionally calmer than a typical conversion-heavy landing page because the decision itself is high-stakes.

## Privacy And Limitations

Landa is a decision-support tool and not a predictor of outcomes. User responses may be stored to improve the product, are not shared with third parties, and should be used alongside official sources and personal judgment. 

Landa is independent of Swedish authorities and universities.

No secrets, API keys, or private configuration are stored in this repository.
