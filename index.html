Privacy and independence

Assessment responses may be stored to improve Landa and are not shared with third parties. Landa is independent and is not affiliated with Swedish authorities or universities.

Decision support, not a verdict

Landa cannot predict outcomes and should not be the sole basis for a mobility decision. Use it alongside official information, lived advice and your own judgment.

      About Landa

      Built for clearer mobility decisions.

      A decision-support service grounded in the realities international students navigate before and after arriving in Sweden.

      The idea

      A more honest view of what it takes to settle.

      Many international students make consequential mobility decisions with guidance that is either generic, overly optimistic, overly pessimistic, or focused only on admission.

      Landa brings financial, employment, language, location and personal conditions into one structured reflection. It does not decide for someone; it helps them see their current position more clearly.

      The readiness model

      Seven connected dimensions.

      The model was calibrated through the judgments of international students and graduates with direct experience of Sweden. No single factor acts as a verdict.

      Tuition security 27%

      How fully tuition is funded at the point of entry.

      Language willingness 17%

      Openness to learning Swedish as part of settling.

      City 16%

      Employment and living conditions in the study location.

      Living-cost buffer 13.5%

      Financial runway for rent, food and everyday expenses.

      Course marketability 11.5%

      Demand for the field within Sweden's labour market.

      Working partner 8%

      Whether a working partner may support the household.

      Personal conviction 7%

      Clarity and confidence behind the mobility decision.

      Designed with clear boundaries.

      Limitations

      A model cannot hold a whole life.

      Landa cannot account for every network, family circumstance, professional history or personal quality. The profile is a starting point for reflection, not a verdict.

      Privacy and data

      Useful insight without exposure.

      Assessment responses may be stored to improve Landa and understand how the service is used. They are not shared with third parties or used to identify people individually.

      What's coming

      Sweden is the starting point.

      Landa is being shaped toward a multi-country mobility platform, with each assessment calibrated to local realities and lived experience.
